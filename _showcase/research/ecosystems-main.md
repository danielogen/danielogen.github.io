    <h5 class="mt-2 mb-2">Software Ecosystems &amp; Dependency Networks</h5>
    <p class="small mb-3">
        Analyzing the Maven Central ecosystem as a network of <strong>1.3M nodes and 20.9M edges</strong>,
        this work reveals scale-free topology and identifies critical infrastructure hubs whose failure
        could cascade across thousands of downstream projects. A companion study examines the
        <a href="https://www.airqo.net/" target="_blank">AirQo</a> data pipeline serving environmental
        monitoring across Africa.
    </p>
