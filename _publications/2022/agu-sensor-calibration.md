abstract: >-
  Low-cost air quality monitors can potentially close air quality data gaps in low-resource settings
  like sub-Saharan Africa where urban environments remain under-monitored. However, low-cost air
  quality sensors are impacted by ambient conditions which affect their data accuracy and thus require
  field calibration. Local calibration methods that require individual collocation of each low-cost
  device with a reference grade monitor are impractical for large networks as they require a lot of
  human resources and time. Additionally, access to reference grade monitors is limited in low-
  resource settings. Machine learning (ML) methods have the potential to support sensor calibration in
  these regions.
