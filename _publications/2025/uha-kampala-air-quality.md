abstract: >-
  Air pollution is a major environmental and public health risk in many African cities. Africa has one
  of the fastest-growing urban populations in the world, and populations living in African urban
  spaces are among the most vulnerable to exposure to dangerous levels of air pollution, with a scale
  higher than other known environmental health challenges, such as sanitation and malnutrition.
  Available evidence shows populations in selected African urban spaces are persistently exposed to
  pollution levels (especially particulate matter, PM2. 5) that exceed the recommended health
  guidelines, often higher than levels recommended by the World Health Organization.
