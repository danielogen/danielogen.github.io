How I teach

Teaching approach

Learning by building

Students learn software engineering best by shipping real products in teams. In senior design I coach teams through requirements, design reviews, sprints, and demos with real stakeholders.

Research in the classroom

I bring findings from my own research on software evolution, refactoring, and AI coding agents into class, so students see how the tools they use every day are studied and improved.

Mentoring

I help students move from coursework to research and start-ups, a path I studied in my FAMECSE 2022 paper on turning undergraduate capstone projects into start-ups.
