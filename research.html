Research

I study how software evolves, how defects propagate through codebases and ecosystems, and how AI is changing the way software is built, through large-scale repository mining, program analysis, and empirical methods. I work in the Software Evolution (EVOL) Lab at UNLV, advised by Prof. John Businge.
